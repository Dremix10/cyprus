Titsu is an online version of Tichu, a four-player partnership card game that is very popular in Greece and Cyprus. There is no real-money gambling, no betting or virtual currency, no in-app purchases and no ads. The app needs an internet connection; games run on our server at aegist.dev.

SIGN-IN IS OPTIONAL
- On launch, type any nickname on the lobby screen and play straight away as a guest (Solo Game, Play Online, Create Room, Join).
- Signing in adds a lasting ELO rating on the Leaderboard, personal stats and recent games, and the Friends list with table invites.
- Demo account: see Sign-In Information (username appreview). To sign in: on the lobby, tap "Sign in" in the row of links at the bottom, enter the username and password, then tap Sign In.
- Sign in with Apple is offered on the same screen (the white "Sign in with Apple" button). Users can also create a username/password account there.

HOW TO TEST ON YOUR OWN
- Fastest: Solo Game > Game length "Quick game" (a single round) > any Bot level > Start. You and a bot partner play against two bots (Zeus, Athena, Apollo). A quick game takes about 3 to 5 minutes. Each round: choose Pass or Call Grand Tichu, tap three cards to pass, then select cards and tap Play, or tap Pass. In solo games a Hint button suggests a play.
- Play Online is matchmaking with other people. If nobody else is searching, the game starts after about a minute and bots take the empty seats. Online games are always to 500 points.
- Private room: Create Room > Create Room shows a four-letter code. Tap Start game and bots fill the empty seats. Inviting a friend needs a second signed-in account on another device, so it is optional for review.
- How to Play (lobby links) is a step-by-step rules guide. Leaderboard is in the same row.
- Language: tap EN or ΕΛ at the top right of the lobby to switch between English and Greek.
- If the connection drops, the app reconnects you to your seat automatically.

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
- While signed in: on the lobby, tap your display name in the row of links at the bottom to open Profile, tap Delete Account, then confirm. Accounts with a password confirm with that password; Sign in with Apple accounts confirm without one. The account, profile, friends and sessions are deleted immediately.
- To keep the demo account available, you may want to test deletion on a new account (Sign in > "Don't have an account? Register"). Deleting the demo account is also fine; we will recreate it.
