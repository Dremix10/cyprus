Titsu is Tichu the way it's played in Cyprus and Greece: four players, two partnerships, fourteen cards each, and plenty of shouting when someone bombs your ace.

Play the full game on your iPhone: online against real players, at a private table with your friends, or solo against bots named after the gods of Olympus.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Play Online: one tap finds you a table for a full game to 500. If the table isn't full after a short wait, bots take the empty seats.
• Private rooms: open a table, share the four-letter room code or invite friends straight from your friends list, and let bots fill any empty seats.
• Solo: partner with a bot against two more. Zeus, Athena and Apollo play at five levels, from Easy to Unfair.
• Your length: in private rooms and solo, pick a Quick game of a single round, or play to 250, 500 or 1000.

THE FULL RULES
• Call Grand Tichu after your first eight cards, or Tichu before your first play.
• Pass one card to each player at the start of every round.
• Every combination: singles, pairs, triples, full houses, straights and runs of pairs.
• Bombs, four of a kind or a straight flush, beat any other play.
• All four special cards, with their Greek names: Ματζόνγκ (Mahjong) and its wish, Σκύλος (Dog), Φοίνικας (Phoenix) and Δράκος (Dragon).
• Scoring is automatic, round by round, with a full score history.

RANKINGS AND FRIENDS
• A leaderboard with ELO rating, peak rating, win rate, Tichu success and double victories.
• Your recent games and personal stats.
• A friends list that shows who's online, with one-tap invites to your table.

BUILT FOR THE TABLE
• Play in Greek or English, and switch any time.
• Lost signal on the way home? Titsu puts you back in your seat.
• The screen stays awake while a game is open.
• New to Tichu? The step-by-step How to Play guide covers every card and combination.

Sign in with Apple, or create an account to keep your rating and friends. Or just type a name and play as a guest.

No ads. No in-app purchases.
